ReadMate Reader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [2026-11-10]

ReadMate Reader ("the app") is a local-first reading app for Windows. Your library, notes and settings stay on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles and when it uses the network.

1. Data stored on your device

  • Your library (book titles, authors, covers, reading progress), highlights, notes, bookmarks and reading statistics are stored in a local database in the app's data folder.
  • App settings (theme, reading preferences, reminders) are stored locally.
  • Books you add from your computer are referenced in place; the app does not upload them anywhere. Reading a book never changes your file, except that annotations you create in a PDF may be written into that PDF file on your device.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal data. The app has no accounts with us, no advertising, and no analytics or telemetry. We operate no server that receives your books, notes or reading history.

3. Cloud sources (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, the app signs you in through that provider's own login page (OAuth). Then:

  • The app requests read access to list and download the book files you pick (for Google Drive: drive.readonly and drive.metadata.readonly).
  • Access tokens are stored on your device only and are never sent to us. You can disconnect at any time in the app; you can also revoke access in your provider's account settings.
  • Files you download are copied into the app's local storage on your device.
  • The provider's own privacy policy applies to data handled on their services.

4. Downloads for offline features (optional)

Translation and audiobook transcription run on your device. When you first use them, the app can download a language or speech model from a public model host (Hugging Face), only after you start the download. Your text and audio are processed locally and are not sent to that host or anyone else. Downloaded models can be removed in Settings → Storage.

5. Other network use

  • Update checks and the optional "import from URL" feature contact the address involved to fetch the file or version information. Such requests include normal technical data (such as your IP address) visible to that server.

6. Notifications and background use

Reading reminders and the system tray option are handled locally on your device using your local time. No reminder data leaves your device.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

8. Your choices

  • Remove your data by deleting books and annotations in the app, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Disconnect cloud sources and delete downloaded models in the app's settings.
